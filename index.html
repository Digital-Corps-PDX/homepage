Digital help from technologists.

In 2025, Portland Digital Corps was a bootstrapped social entrepreneurship founded by Ron Bronson. PDXDC asked local technologists to contribute their skills to local nonprofits and other groups.

The plan was always to run a short 8 week sprint to help as many orgs as we could. We ended up going most of the year, thanks to our volunteers and completed every project we accepted during our sprint.

Over 100 people answered our call to participate.

Together they gave more than 5,500 volunteer hours to work that has reached thousands of people well beyond Oregon and will leave a lasting model for how to scale impact rapidly.
